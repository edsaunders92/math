## Chapter 1A — Exercise 1

**Problem.** Show that $\alpha + \beta = \beta + \alpha$ for all $\alpha, \beta \in \mathbb{C}$.

**Proof.**

By definition, let $\alpha = a + bi$ and $\beta = c + di$ for $a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{R}$.

Then

$$
\begin{aligned}
\alpha + \beta
&= (a + bi) + (c + di) \\
&= (a + c) + (b + d)i && \text{(by definition)} \\
&= (c + a) + (d + b)i && \text{(as } a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{R}\text{)} \\
&= (c + di) + (a + bi) && \text{(by definition)} \\
&= \beta + \alpha
\end{aligned}
$$

$\square$
